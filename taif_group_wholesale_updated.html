Wholesale FAQ

How can I become a Taif Group wholesale partner?

Complete the partner application. Our team reviews your business details and contacts you.

Who can open a wholesale account?

Retailers, distributors, perfume stores, wholesalers, corporate buyers and international partners.

How can I access wholesale prices?

Prices are shown inside the Wholesale Portal once your account is approved.

Why are product prices not displayed on the website?

Wholesale terms vary by partner and market, so pricing is shared only with approved accounts.

Can I distribute Taif Group brands in my country?

Tell us your market in the application. Availability is reviewed per country.

Which brands are available for wholesale?

Taif Al Emarat, Ramasat, Cunzite and Prima Chic.

Can I order from multiple brands?

Yes. One account can order across the portfolio.

Is there a minimum wholesale order?

Minimums are confirmed to approved partners in the portal.

Do you provide international shipping?

Shipping options are confirmed during account approval, by destination.

Can I become an exclusive distributor?

Exclusive arrangements are discussed case by case. Mention it in your application.

How long does account approval take?

Timing depends on how complete your business details are. The team will confirm after you apply.

How can I contact the wholesale team?

Use the contact form below, email or WhatsApp.
